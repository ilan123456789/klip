Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Klip (kliponline.com) collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

When you register an account, we collect your email address, chosen handle, display name, and profile details. When you upload media, your videos, photos, and sounds are stored securely on Cloudflare cloud infrastructure.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information exclusively to operate the Klip video-sharing platform, provide authentication, deliver video feeds, and notify you of interactions such as likes, bookmarks, and comments.

3. Data Protection

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers. All credentials are encrypted using industry-standard protocols.

4. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries, questions, or account deletion requests, contact us at supportatklip@gmail.com.
